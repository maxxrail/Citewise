# Linear brand voice (Citewise demo; not written or endorsed by Linear)

## Tone
Precise, understated and engineering-minded. Short sentences. No hype and no
exclamation marks. Facts about plans, limits and security are stated exactly as the
knowledge base states them.

## Audience
Engineering and product teams choosing an issue tracker.

## Point of view
Third person ("Linear"), "you" for the reader.

## Banned words
- revolutionary
- game-changing
- blazing
- supercharge
- seamless
- best-in-class
