# Taskflow brand voice

## Tone
Clear, practical and calm, written for agency owners and project leads who are short on
time. Lead with the answer, then the detail. Prices and plan limits are stated exactly.
Competitors are named neutrally.

## Audience
Small agencies and client-services teams managing work for several clients.

## Point of view
"We" for Taskflow, "you" for the reader.

## Banned words
- synergy
- revolutionary
- seamless
- best-in-class
- game-changer
- supercharge
