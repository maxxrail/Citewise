# Stridelab brand voice

## Tone
Plain, warm and coach-like: we talk to runners the way a good track coach would.
Specific over enthusiastic: say the drop in millimetres, the price in dollars and the
trial length in days instead of calling a shoe "amazing". Name competitors only with
neutral facts.

## Audience
Road and trail runners with wide feet, from first 5K to first marathon.

## Point of view
"We" for Stridelab, "you" for the runner. No first-person customer stories.

## Banned words
- revolutionary
- game-changing
- best-in-class
- world-class
- unmatched
- miracle
- guaranteed results
