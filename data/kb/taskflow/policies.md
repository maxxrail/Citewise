# Taskflow trials, billing and security

## Free trial

New workspaces get a 14-day free trial of the Business plan. No credit card is required.

## Refunds

Annual plans can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase. Monthly plans are not refunded.

## Security

Taskflow is SOC 2 Type II audited. Customers can choose to store data in the United States or the European Union.
