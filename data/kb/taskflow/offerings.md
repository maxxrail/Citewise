# Taskflow plans, pricing and integrations

## Plans and pricing

Taskflow has three plans. The Free plan is for up to 3 users and includes unlimited projects and 1 GB of file storage.

The Pro plan costs $12 per user per month billed annually, or $15 per user per month billed monthly. Pro adds unlimited users, timelines and the client portal.

The Business plan costs $24 per user per month billed annually. Business adds advanced reporting, SAML single sign-on and audit logs.

## Client portal

The client portal gives clients guest access to selected projects. It is included on the paid plans (Pro and Business) and is not available on the Free plan.

## Apps

Taskflow runs in the browser and has desktop apps for macOS and Windows and mobile apps for iOS and Android.

## Integrations and importers

Taskflow integrates with Slack, GitHub, Google Drive, Figma and Zapier. It has built-in importers for Jira, Asana and Trello, so projects can be moved over with their tasks and comments.
