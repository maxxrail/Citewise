# About Taskflow

Taskflow is a fictional project management tool used for Citewise demos. It was founded in 2020 in Toronto, Canada, by Priya Raman and Owen Brooks, and has about 60 employees.

## Customers

Taskflow is used by more than 4,000 teams, most of them agencies and small software companies.
