# Stridelab returns, trial and warranty

## 60-day trial

Stridelab offers a 60-day trial on all shoes, worn or unworn. Returns within the trial are free in the United States and Canada and are refunded to the original payment method.

## Warranty

Stridelab shoes come with a 6-month warranty against manufacturing defects. The warranty does not cover normal wear of the outsole or upper.

## Shipping

Shipping is free on orders over $50. Stridelab ships to the United States and Canada only.
