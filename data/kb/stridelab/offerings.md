# Stridelab shoes and prices

## Current models

Stridelab currently makes three models, all available in standard and wide widths, in US men's sizes 7 to 15 and women's sizes 5 to 12.

The Stridelab Tempo is a lightweight daily trainer. It costs $140, weighs 240 grams (men's size 9) and has an 8 mm heel-to-toe drop.

The Stridelab Trail is a trail shoe with a 4 mm lug outsole for grip on wet rock. It costs $155, weighs 290 grams and has a 6 mm drop. The Trail line launched in 2023 and is still in production.

The Stridelab Long Run is a max-cushion shoe for marathon training. It costs $165, weighs 275 grams and has a 10 mm drop.

## Fit

All Stridelab models have a wide, foot-shaped toe box. The wide width adds 4 mm of width at the forefoot.
