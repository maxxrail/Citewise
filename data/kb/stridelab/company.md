# About Stridelab

Stridelab is a fictional direct-to-consumer running shoe brand used for Citewise demos. It was founded in 2021 in Portland, Oregon, by Maya Chen and Luis Ortega, two former track coaches.

## Where Stridelab sells

Stridelab sells through its own website, stridelab.example, and through about 40 independent specialty running stores in the United States and Canada. Stridelab does not sell on Amazon or through any other online marketplace.

## Manufacturing

Stridelab shoes are designed in Portland and manufactured in Vietnam by a partner factory. Uppers use at least 50% recycled polyester.
