# Stridelab and other running shoe brands

Stridelab competes with Nike, Hoka, Brooks, ASICS, New Balance and On. Like Altra it uses a foot-shaped toe box, but unlike Altra's zero-drop shoes every Stridelab model has a conventional drop of 6 to 10 mm. Stridelab is smaller than these brands and sells mainly direct to consumers.
