# Linear security and compliance

## Certifications

Linear undergoes SOC 2 Type II audits and has ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification. Linear states it is committed to GDPR compliance. HIPAA compliance is available on the Enterprise plan, and Linear offers a BAA on request.

## Data residency and encryption

When creating a workspace, customers choose to store their data in the European Union or the United States. Data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.2 and at rest with AES 256-bit encryption.

## Single sign-on

Google sign-in is available on the Free, Basic and Business plans. SAML single sign-on, with identity providers such as Okta and OneLogin, is available on the Enterprise plan only.
