# About Linear

Linear was founded in 2019. According to its about page, Linear has become the tool of choice for more than 50,000 companies, including OpenAI, Coinbase and Ramp.

## Leadership

Linear's co-founders are Karri Saarinen (CEO), Jori Lallo (CPO) and Tuomas Artman (CTO). Cristina Cordova is COO.

## Team

Linear's team is distributed across North America and Europe.

## Investors

Linear's about page lists partners from Accel and Sequoia among its backers, along with individual backers such as Dylan Field (CEO of Figma) and Patrick Collison (CEO of Stripe). The page does not state funding amounts or rounds.
