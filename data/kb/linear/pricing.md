# Linear plans and pricing

## Plans

Linear has four plans: Free, Basic, Business and Enterprise.

The Free plan costs $0 and is free for everyone. It includes unlimited members, 2 teams, 250 issues and file uploads up to 10 MB.

The Basic plan costs $10 per user per month, billed yearly. It includes 5 teams, unlimited issues, unlimited file uploads and admin roles.

The Business plan costs $16 per user per month, billed yearly. It includes unlimited teams, private teams and guests, Triage Intelligence, Code Intelligence, Linear Insights, Linear Asks, and the Zendesk and Intercom integrations.

The Enterprise plan has custom pricing with annual billing only. It adds invoice and purchase-order billing, SAML and SCIM, granular admin controls, priority support and account management.

## Monthly and yearly billing

Free, Basic and Business can be billed monthly or yearly. The Enterprise plan is billed yearly only. The pricing page shows per-user prices for yearly billing.

## API limits

API rate limits are standard on Free and Basic, 2 times standard on Business, and 5 times standard on Enterprise.

## Features included on every plan

Every plan includes issues, projects, cycles and initiatives, API and webhook access, import and export, triage, Pulse, issue sync, MCP access, Linear Agent, Loops, progress reports and the Microsoft Teams integration.

## Business-only and Enterprise-only features

Issue SLAs, triage rules, sub-teams (one level), guest accounts, Insights, Slack intake and email intake require the Business plan or higher. Dashboards, SCIM provisioning, IP restrictions, an audit log, HIPAA compliance, an uptime SLA and sub-teams up to five levels deep are Enterprise only.
