# Linear billing, refunds and discounts

## How billing works

Linear bills for the number of unsuspended users in a workspace. On yearly plans, adding users creates a pro-rated charge and suspending users creates a pro-rated credit; credits are applied to future invoices and are not refunded.

## Refunds and cancellation

Invoices are final once issued. Linear does not provide refunds for cancellations mid-subscription. A cancellation takes effect at the end of the billing period; a workspace with more than 250 issues can then no longer create new issues.

## Discounts

Non-profits get 75% off the Basic and Business plans; the discount is not available on Enterprise. University staff get a 75% education discount on Basic and Business, and full-time students get free access for one year for student-led programs. Startups in Linear's Startup Program partner network can get up to 6 months of free access to the Business plan.
